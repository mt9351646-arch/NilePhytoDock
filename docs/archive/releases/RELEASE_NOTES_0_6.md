NilePhytoDock Studio 0.6.0 is developed by Ahmed G Soliman and Malak Taha.

This verified Windows release adds atom-verified interactions, Classic/Circular/Functional group diagrams, independent residue colors, native 3D appearance controls, SMARTS participation, Morgan/Tanimoto and site-specific interaction similarity, sixteen scientific charts, six interaction-export modes and portable saved projects. The supplied emblem is embedded in the EXE and seven-size ICO; all application text is English and the English manuals are external.

The source-qualified seed contains 57 botanical oils, 119 analytical profiles and 2109 observations. Its 819 normalized names include 186 ready name records representing 164 distinct InChIKeys; 633 remain non-ready. Fixed-oil pools, analytical derivatives, uncertain stereochemistry, non-Egyptian reference samples and historical evidence retain explicit qualifications. Earlier raw observations are preserved.

Validation: 56 automated tests, with ten affected diagram tests repeated after the final SVG correction, native WebGL/Plotly and cross-format exports, real P2Rank and three fresh Smina jobs in the final frozen EXE. The actual 3PTB/BEN controls achieved approximately 0.39 Angstrom heavy-atom RMSD without alignment. The botanical case retains 69 poses and 1917 independently verified contacts. It has low composition coverage and is not evidence of whole-oil efficacy, safety or synergy; no unmatched AUROC/affinity correlation is claimed. Windows 11 on the development host is tested. Windows 10, clean VM and all GPUs are untested; EXE is unsigned.
